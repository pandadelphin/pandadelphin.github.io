SKATERBOY / Controls

Touch / smartphone
LS / RS circles: front / rear foot; drag from the center.
Stick trails show your latest gesture and fade automatically. Buttons light up while held; trigger meters show pressure.
LT / RT thumb zones: slide left on LT, right on RT for more pressure, release to let go. Enable optional tilt steering in Settings. Hold both triggers to prepare, then release both to pump; or hold/release either PUMP button with one thumb.
B above LT: brake. A above RT: push. LB / RB: catch. D-pad down / up: set / return to marker.
Ollie: hold RS down, then flick LS up. Move LS left then up for a kickflip, right then up for a heelflip. Scoop RS from down to the side for a 180 shuvit, or continue to up for a 360, then pop. Reverse feet for a nollie.
On a rollback, the same gestures become fakie (RS down then LS up) or switch (LS up then RS down).
In air, move LS left / RS right (or the reverse) to turn the board and feet sideways up to 90 degrees. Release to straighten.
Drag the uncovered park to orbit; pinch to zoom. Landscape gives more room. Tap Y to cycle frontside, backside and classic cameras.

Gamepad (standard Xbox / PlayStation)
A / Cross or X / Square: push. B / Circle: mongo push. L3: brake.
LT / L2 and RT / R2: steer; in air: body spin.
Hold both triggers, then release: pump on curved ramps up to 45 degrees from horizontal, or near a straight bank's top edge. Holding alone adds no speed.
Keep right stick DOWN, flick left stick UP: ollie.
While rolling backward: RS down then LS up for fakie; LS up then RS down for switch. The sticks stay with the same feet.
After preparing, flick front foot left: kickflip; right: heelflip.
Scoop rear stick from down to the side for a 180 shuvit, or continue to up for a 360, then pop.
In air, stick left/right tweaks the board and feet sideways up to 90 degrees; front/rear directions are mirrored. Up/down adjusts pitch. Release to straighten.
Reverse feet for nollies. Bumpers: catch (auto-catch also enabled).
D-pad DOWN: set marker. UP: return.
Y / Triangle: frontside (default), backside, classic camera. Low cameras keep their side when rolling fakie.

Keyboard
W push / S brake / A,D steer or spin.
Hold Space, release: ollie. Hold Q/E before release: flip.
Hold Z/C before release: 180 shuvit. Ctrl adjusts flip strength.
Hold/release Shift: pump. G: catch. M: set marker. R: return.
I,J,K,L = front foot; arrow keys = rear foot.
Mouse drag: orbit. Wheel: zoom.

Land flat and aligned to roll away. Ollie onto a ledge edge or rail aligned for a grind, sideways for a slide. Hold a foot up/down for nose/tail contacts. Ollie out and link tricks before landing. Open Map & lines for the pump circuit, stair plaza and mega ramp.

SKATERBOY / MAKE IT YOURS

Settings

Your ride. Your way to control it.

Tilt steering

Turn using your phone's gyroscope. Tilt left or right from your comfortable holding angle. Touch triggers still work for steering, spins and pumping.

Use the outward LT / RT sliders to steer.

Controller vibration

Successful pumps give a short rumble. Support depends on your controller, browser and connection; for Stadia, try USB if Bluetooth does not vibrate.

Press a controller button, then test vibration.
